### PROFILE CLASSIFICATION
Use exactly one of these labels in profile_classification:
1. COMMON_PERSON
A regular personal profile with no clear signals of business, service selling, commercial acquisition, or professional activity.

- No business phone.
- No professional email.
- No services offered.
2. INDEPENDENT_NICHE_SPECIALIST
A person offering services or working in a niche, but appearing independent, informal, or unclear whether they represent a company.
Examples:
- "I install fences, inbox me"
- "Hago cercas"
- "Trabajo haciendo patios y cercas"
- "I do handyman work"
- "Independent realtor"
- Services offered from a personal profile without a clear business brand.
3. COMPANY_AFFILIATED_PROFESSIONAL
A professional person affiliated with a company, broker, brand, agency, construction company, real estate company, or business, but without clear evidence of ownership.
Signals:

- Recognizable company logo.
- Professional signature.
- "Realtor at..."

- "Sales representative"
- "Project manager at..."
- "Works at..."

Important:
- This does NOT automatically mean business owner.
- If the profile says "Realtor - Coldwell Banker Realty", classify here, not as LOCAL_BUSINESS_OWNER.
4. LOCAL_BUSINESS_OWNER
A person who clearly appears to be the owner, founder, director, or controller of a local business.
Strong signals:
- "Owner"
- "Founder"
- "CEO"
- "President"
- "My company"
- "My business"
- "Owned by"
- Personal name associated with their own LLC.
- Official business page where the person presents themselves as owner.
- Posts where they speak as the business owner.
5. LOCAL_BUSINESS_PAGE
A profile or page that directly represents a local business, not an individual person.
Signals:
- Profile name is a company name.
- Commercial category.
- Own logo.
- Business phone.
- Business website.
- Direct sales posts.
- Local brand operating in Chicago or surrounding areas.
6. NON_LOCAL_OR_UNRELATED_BUSINESS
A commercial or professional profile that does not appear to operate locally in Chicago/surrounding areas or is not usefully related to the evaluated niche.
7. UNKNOWN
Insufficient or ambiguous data.
